\section*{F1. The $-\log2$ bound is against EW, not the best window}
$\text{hEW}\ge\frac12\text{EW}$ gives $\log\text{hEW}\ge\log\text{EW}-\log2$, which is what your docstring and \texttt{he\_worst} measure. EW is not $\ge$ the best window. On your seeds:

The true pointwise guarantees are $\log Z\ge\log\text{EW}-\log2$ and $\log Z\ge\log M_w-\log16$ ($-2.773$) for every window $w$. The second holds in every cell. \section*{F3. ``MixWin dominates'' is the same event as ``half-EW beats EW''}
Let $\bar W=\frac18\sum M_w$ and $Z_c=c\,\text{EW}+(1-c)\bar W$. Then $\text{EW}=Z_1$, $\text{hEW}=Z_{1/2}$ and $\text{MW}=Z_{1/9}$. On each seed $Z_c-Z_{c'}=(c-c')(\text{EW}-\bar W)$, so
\[\operatorname{sign}(\text{MW}-\text{hEW})=\operatorname{sign}(\text{hEW}-\text{EW})\quad\text{on every seed}.\]
I verified this on 2000/2000 seeds in every cell I ran, and your JSON has \texttt{mw\_cliff}$=$\texttt{he\_cliff} in all 22 cells. In REVERSED $\gamma\le0.10$ MixWin loses to hEW on most seeds ($\delta=-0.297\dots-0.084$) even where its mean leads. Since $\log Z_c$ is concave in $c$, the real \S5b question is $c^\ast=\arg\max_c\mathbb E\log Z_c$, with the guarantee $\log Z_c\ge\max(\log\text{EW}+\log c,\ \log M_w+\log\frac{1-c}{8})$.

\section*{Your 957 questions}
\textbf{(a) Per-item clip.} Valid in \texttt{accumulate\_wealth}, where each factor is one item and $U-V-\delta\ge-(1+\delta_t)$. No cross-cell cancellation is involved. You need only $\delta_t\in\mathcal F_{t-1}$, the same condition the slack needs, so use the a-priori $\varepsilon_k$, not realised $p$'s. In the block combiners one factor sums over strata. There the condition is $\sum_s\lambda_s(1+\delta_{m,s})\le1$, and the cap $1/b$ with $b=1+\max\delta$ suffices. Per-stratum clips would not.

\textbf{(b) Starvation} (speculative, not run). Decouple pairing from betting strata. In each block, sort by $\theta$ ($\mathcal F_0$) and pair neighbours. Each pair is a cell with $n=2$: $\mathbb E S=\frac12\Delta p_i\Delta p_j\le\frac12R_iR_j$, and both partners are fresh, so Prop.~3 validity is unchanged. Assign each pair to the stratum of its midpoint for the bet weights. That gives 10 pairs per block at any $Q$, and neighbour gaps of about $1/21$ give a tighter slack than an equal-width bin when $Q<21$.

\textbf{(c) Which \S5b.} Derive it. Equal-width bins plus (A$_L$) give $\varepsilon_k\le L/(2Q)$ and $\delta_k\le L^2/(2Q^2)$. With $L=\frac12$ that is $1/(4Q)$, the CSV's $\bar\varepsilon$. ``$\varepsilon_k\le(\max-\min)/2$'' is a tautology, since $\varepsilon_k$ is the half-range.
