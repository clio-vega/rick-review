\emph{Q2.} $\varepsilon_k=(\max-\min)/2$ of probabilities in $[0,1]$, so $\varepsilon_k\in[0,\tfrac12]$ always. A single-item stratum gives $\varepsilon_k=0$. The bound $b\le2$ is therefore universal, unless estimated probabilities from an unclipped estimator are plugged in. Clip them to $[0,1]$.
